Codex/user should only execute genuinely unavailable capabilities with necessary authorization: dependency fetching not accessible here; Docker, live PostgreSQL/Maven builds where absent; browser/device matrix; IAM, AWS and DNS account provisioning; external provider APIs and payment tests. Legal/provider acceptance, participant age/consent proof and independent security testing require actual authorized external reviewers—not a coding assistant.

**Work queues are assignments, NOT completed-task status.** Prepared integrations remain prepared until run, and partial work remains partial until accepted. No public content is authorized or deployed.
